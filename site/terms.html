Terms of service

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Peakreel is a tool operated for our own channels; it has no outside users. These terms describe how we use it and how we treat the creators whose streams we clip.

Our use of streams

  • We clip only public Twitch streams, and only short moments.
  • Every clip credits its streamer on the video and links to their channel.
  • Every clip is reviewed by a person before it is posted.
  • We do not edit clips to misrepresent what happened, and we do not use them to advertise anything.

Creators' rights

Streamers own their content. Any streamer can ask us to stop clipping them, or to remove any clip, at any time, and we comply without asking for a reason. See data deletion.

Platforms

Clips are posted to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, under each platform's own terms, including the YouTube Terms of Service.

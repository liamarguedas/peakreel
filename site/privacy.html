Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Peakreel is a tool run by its operator to make and post highlight clips from public Twitch streams. It is not a service that other people sign up for. This page explains what data it handles and why.

What we collect

  • Public Twitch content: the video and audio of public live streams we choose to follow, and their public chat messages (with the chatter's public username). Chat is used only to detect moments worth clipping and to show the reviewer what viewers reacted to.
  • Our own platform accounts: when we connect our own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, the platforms give us access tokens. These are stored in the operating system's credential store on our computer, and are used only to upload our clips to those same accounts.

We do not collect data from people who watch our clips, we do not use cookies or analytics on this site, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Recorded stream video is kept for about 10 minutes on a rolling basis, and only clipped moments are saved.
  • Clips that are not approved are deleted after 2 to 7 days. Approved clips are kept so they can be posted.
  • Chat logs are deleted after 90 days.
  • Platform access tokens are kept until we disconnect the account, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings.

Use of Google, YouTube and Meta data

Peakreel uses the YouTube Data API and Meta's Instagram and Facebook Graph APIs only to upload videos to, and read the upload status of, accounts that we own. By using YouTube features, you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to Peakreel can be revoked at Google account permissions and in Facebook's settings under Business integrations.

Streamers and people who appear in clips

If you are a streamer and do not want your content clipped, or you appear in a clip and want it removed, see data deletion. We act on every request, and we review each clip for personal information such as names in on-screen alerts before posting it.

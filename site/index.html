Peakreel

Highlights from live streams, credited to their creators.

Peakreel watches public Twitch streams, notices when a chat suddenly reacts to something, and turns those moments into short vertical clips with captions. A person reviews every clip before it is posted.

How clips are made

  • Only public live streams and public chat are used.
  • Every clip names its streamer on the video and links to their Twitch channel in the text.
  • Clips are posted to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, one set per language.
  • Nothing is posted without a human review; clips with music, alerts showing viewer names, or flagged words are checked first.

For streamers

If you do not want your streams clipped, tell us and we stop immediately: your channel is added to our block list, recording stops, unpublished clips are deleted, and published clips are taken down. See data deletion for how to reach us.

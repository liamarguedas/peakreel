Data deletion and removal requests

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Streamers: stop clipping, remove clips

Email sodecapital@gmail.com from an address we can link to your channel (or message us from your channel), with the subject "Removal". We do not ask for a reason.

When we receive a request we:

  1. add your channel to our block list, which within a minute stops recording your stream and logging your chat;
  2. delete your buffered video and every clip not yet posted;
  3. remove the clips already posted on our YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts;
  4. confirm to you when it is done.

Anyone appearing in a clip

If a clip shows your name, a message you sent, or anything else about you, email us with a link to the clip and we remove it.

Facebook and Instagram data

Peakreel connects only to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that we own, and stores no data about other Facebook or Instagram users. If you believe Peakreel holds data about you, email us and we delete it, or remove Peakreel's access in Facebook's settings under Business integrations.
